Get Started

1

Create a Wallet

Download Metamask or Trustwallet and create a wallet. add the Binance Smart Chain to your network-list.

2

Get BNB

Buy BNB on an exchange (i.e. BSC). Transfer BNB to your wallet address. BNB addresses start with a “0x".

3

Connect Your Wallet

At the top of the site, click "Connect Wallet", confirm the action, after which you should see the numbers of your wallet.

4

Make A Deposit

On the dashboard, click on the "Deposit" button. In the window that opens, enter the amount, click again on the "Deposit" button and confirm the action.

5

Get Dividends!

You have successfully created a deposit! Now every minute you will receive dividends that you can withdraw once a day by clicking on the "Withdraw daily" button.

FAQ

What is the minimum amount of BNB I can deposit ?

Minimum deposit is 0.5 bnb. You can make a deposit of more than 0.5 BNB to begin with. And don’t worry, You can make as many deposits as you please.

Is FinToCh safe?:

Yes, FinToCh has a safe and verified smart contract on BSC network with no backdoor, no one can change it. it has been developed by powerful team

How much am I paying in fees?

There is 5% dev fee taken upon ALL deposits and withdraw from FinToCh. The only fee that users personally incur is their transaction gas fees, which is charged by the BSC network.

Can I make multiple deposits?

Yes. You can make as many number of deposits as you like. We encourage that as increases your returns accordingly.

Can I make daily withdrawals?

The amount of daily profit is 2% per day and its forever as lifetime, which must be withdrawn every 24 hours. The daily profit is calculated based on the sum of your total deposit and your reinvestment bonus, and it must be withdrawn every day and is not transferred to the next day. Your next withdrawal time is 24 hour after the previous harvest

referral rewards

Anyone can receive referral rewards up to 10 times of their total deposit, if amount of your referral rewards exceeds 10 times of your total deposit, you cannot withdraw unless you increase your deposit.

What is 5 Level Referral Program?

Connect your wallet and complete a deposit to FinToCh. Once the transaction is processed, you will get a custom link at ‘Your Referral Link:’. Copy it and share with your friends or fellow crypto enthusiasts. There are 5 levels of referral rewards, if someone invests with your referral link, you will get 30% bonus, total up to 30% bonus paid in 5 levels(20% , 5% , 3% , 1% , 1%)

How to use FinToCh on my phone?

Simply use any browser that you want and choose Metamask or Trustwallet to connect to your wallet's app on your phone
